Work it, check it, then say it in your own words

Concepts that are hard to hold on to from a formula alone, turned into something you can operate. Each demo keeps to one question: change one condition and compare, restate the reason, then see whether the explanation still holds for a different example.

About this site

  • Free to open. No account and no other purchase is a condition of learning here.
  • No advertising, no analytics tags, and nothing is sent anywhere. Input is handled inside the browser.
  • Each demo states its assumed background, its goal and the limits of its model. No claim is made about curriculum alignment or exam results.
  • No check of learning effect has been carried out. What is published here is a draft of the material.

Reading the code

The demos are built on JavaScript libraries split by purpose. Each folder documents how to use it.

  • js/core — random numbers and number handling
  • js/linear-algebra — matrices in homogeneous coordinates
  • js/clustering — the k-means procedure
  • js/sorting — twenty-five sorting procedures
  • js/viz — drawing the plane, the scatter plot and the bar chart
  • js/ui — controls, tables and wording
