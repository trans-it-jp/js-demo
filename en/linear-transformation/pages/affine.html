Linear transform and translation combined

A linear transform L composed with a translation T. It is built as T·L, so a point sees L first. Check what changes when the order is swapped.

The question
Does “scale then move” give the same result as “move then scale”?
Assumed background
Coordinates, and multiplying tables of numbers (matrices). Written for upper-secondary level and above.
Predict before you run it
With sx at 2 and tx at 3, where do you expect the point [1, 0] to land?
Draw Click or drag on the figure to draw. Anything you draw is transformed by the same matrix.

The dashed outline is the original shape and the solid one is the result. The pale grid shows where the cells go.

Things to check

  • Compare: compare the tables for L and T against the third column of the combined matrix.
  • Explain: say why the third column of the combined matrix matches the shift in T.
  • Transfer: with sx=2 and tx=3, work out the image of [1, 0] by hand and check it against the figure.

What this page covers

  • Affine transforms only. The third row of the matrix is always [0, 0, 1]; projective transforms are not included.
  • Shapes can only be drawn with a pointer, not from the keyboard. Every parameter can also be set in a number field.
  • No check of learning effect has been carried out. This page is a draft of the material.
